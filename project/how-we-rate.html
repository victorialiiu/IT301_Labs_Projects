How we rate

Each cafe is scored from 1 to 5 in three categories. The overall rating is the average, rounded to one decimal.

Coffee

Taste, temperature and consistency.

What earns a 5?

Balanced flavor and a drink that tastes the same every visit.

Atmosphere

Seating, noise, lighting and cleanliness.

What earns a 5?

Comfortable seats and a noise level that fits the cafe’s purpose.

Service

Speed and friendliness of staff.

What earns a 5?

Fast orders and a warm welcome, even when busy.
